arXiv API Terms of Use Notice
==============================
Recorded: 2026-11-25
Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/index.html

All arXiv metadata and abstracts retrieved by AI Research Pulse respect the arXiv API Terms of Use
(rate limit <= 1 request per 3 seconds, canonical attribution & direct links to https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>
and https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>.pdf, abstract-only indexing without full-paper redistribution).
Users should always check the license of individual arXiv preprints before downstream reuse.
